# Approved style library

Choose a style only after the mental-state metaphor and layout logic are clear. Keep style independent from layout.

1. `水墨未来` — Black ink congestion opening into a warm, precise path. Best for overthinking, verification, and release.
2. `手绘动画` — Pastoral, hand-painted animation warmth. Best for recovery, hope, and gentle momentum. Describe it generically; do not imitate a living artist by name.
3. `像素 RPG` — Premium 32-bit role-playing-game world. Best for turning a difficult day into one playable quest.
4. `新艺术星象` — Art Nouveau botanical lines, brass, stained glass, and Libra balance. Best for boundaries, elegance, and decision-making.
5. `复古掌机` — A tactile handheld-console interface and hardware frame. Best for playful self-tracking and compact status dashboards.
6. `微缩世界` — Handcrafted diorama with miniature objects. Best for making complex systems feel understandable and controllable.
7. `漫画分镜` — Editorial graphic-novel panels with clear sequence. Best for inner dialogue and before/after narratives.
8. `复古科幻` — Analog orbital navigator, printed route cards, and one selected trajectory. Best for repeated loops turning into a concrete route.
9. `疗愈绘本` — Adult picture-book tactility, paper boats, rain, and sunrise. Best for fatigue, self-permission, and rest.

Exclude dreamcore surrealism, cinematic mental-weather, tactile transition, and paper/collage by default. Do not include them in option sets or website libraries unless the user explicitly opts back in.

## Option design

When the user says “I do not know what I want”:

- Offer three to six options with distinct metaphors.
- Keep the same verified copy and widget content across options so the visual difference is easy to judge.
- Name the difference in one short phrase, then show the complete visual.
- Preserve all options the user approves.
